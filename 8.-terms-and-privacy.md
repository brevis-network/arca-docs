# Privacy, Anonymization and Terms

## How Arca Protects Your Data

AI sessions can contain sensitive information in prompts, responses, code snippets, and tool outputs. Arca automatically processes confirmed submissions to reduce the exposure of common credentials and personal identifiers while preserving useful workflow context.

After you confirm a submission and it passes duplicate checks, Arca processes the session in the background and creates a separate, sanitized copy. Processing can begin while the submission is under review; it does not need to wait for final approval.

Arca applies two types of protection:

* **Sensitive credential removal:** Detected credentials, such as API keys, access tokens, private keys, seed phrases, and database passwords, are replaced with redaction markers.
* **Personal identifier masking:** Supported personal identifiers, such as email addresses, usernames in local file paths, public IP addresses, and certain financial or government identifiers, are replaced with placeholders.

Once processing is complete, you can download the sanitized session from the [Contributor Dashboard](7.-contributor-dashboard-guide.md#track-submissions) and review what was removed or replaced. Sanitized files are not publicly accessible. You must sign in to access your own submission, and downloads use temporary links.

Anonymization creates a separate copy. **It does not automatically delete or overwrite your original submission.** Retention and use of original and processed data remain governed by the [Privacy Policy](https://arca.market/privacy_policy.pdf) and [Terms of Service](https://arca.market/tos.pdf).

## What Anonymization Does Not Guarantee

Arca’s data anonymization reduces privacy risks, but it does not guarantee complete anonymity or the removal of every sensitive detail.

* **Detection is not exhaustive.** Unusual formats, encoded content, or sensitive information expressed in natural language may not be detected. Some non-sensitive content may also be masked.
* **Context can still reveal identity.** Project names, code, business details, and other remaining context may make a person or organization identifiable.
* **Repository archives are not covered by this session-cleaning process.** A sanitized session does not mean that a separately uploaded repository has also been sanitized.
* **Original data is still processed by Arca.** Anonymization happens after upload and confirmation, not before Arca receives the files.
* **Verification is not anonymization.** Authenticity proofs help verify AI interactions; they do not, by themselves, remove personal information or make the content private.

## Your Choices and Responsibilities

Only submit data that you own or are authorized to contribute. Before uploading, review your content for credentials, third-party personal data, and confidential employer or client information. Do not rely on automatic anonymization to make otherwise unauthorized content suitable for submission.
