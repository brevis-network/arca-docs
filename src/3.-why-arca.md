# 3. Why Arca

AI-assisted work creates more than a final output. It also creates a record of how people prompt models, explore ideas, debug problems, use tools, and iterate toward a result.

This workflow data can be valuable, but it is difficult to evaluate and reward fairly:

* Public code and discussions provide limited new insight because they are already widely available.
* Final outputs rarely reveal the prompts, failed attempts, debugging steps, and AI-assisted decisions behind them.
* Session data may be duplicated, incomplete, manipulated, or unrelated to the submitted work.
* Private workflows provide richer context but may contain sensitive prompts, responses, tool outputs, code, and repo content.

## Arca's Approach

Arca enables Contributors to securely submit private AI coding workflows in privacy-preserving way:

* Selected data is anonymized before upload.
* Arca applies authenticity, eligibility, duplication, and quality checks to each submission.
* Public, duplicate, incomplete or ineligible submissions, and submissions with too little real AI interaction, may be rejected.
